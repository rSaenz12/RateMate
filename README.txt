## Description: 
RateMate is a web-based application that bridges the gap between raw review platforms like Yelp and actionable insights for both consumers and businesses. 
By integrating Yelp’s API, custom sentiment analysis, and AI-powered summarization, RateMate transforms unstructured customer reviews into clear summaries and sentiment breakdowns. 
Users can quickly make informed decisions with concise, location-based results, while businesses gain deeper intelligence from customer feedback. 
Designed for accuracy, performance, and ease of use, RateMate delivers faster decision-making for consumers and smarter insights for businesses.
